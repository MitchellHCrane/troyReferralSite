Frequently Asked Questions

How much do I earn for referring a loan officer?

Realtors earn $25 for any referral who interviews with First Class Home Mortgage. You earn $695 for a referral who is hired, stays in their position, and closes at least one loan with us. The maximum is 10 referrals per month, or $7,200.

Who is eligible to be referred?

Currently licensed, active loan officers who have an NMLS number.

Which states does the program cover?

First Class Home Mortgage is licensed and actively hiring in California, Colorado, Idaho, New Mexico, Texas, Utah, and Florida.

How do I submit a referral?

Use the online referral form linked on this page — it takes about two minutes. If two or more people refer the same candidate, only the first referrer receives the reward.

Is there a cap on how many referrals I can make?

Yes. A Realtor can make up to 10 referrals per month, for a maximum of $7,200.

What is the loan officer compensation plan?

Loan officers have a maximum comp plan of 2% lender-paid per closed loan. A 401(k) is offered to tenured employees.

Who runs the program and who do I contact?

The Partner/Recruiting Referral Program is run by CEO Troy Warner. Call (801) 597-8832 or email loans@troywarner.com for details.
